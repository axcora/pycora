Why Python Became the Most Popular Programming Language in 2026

Introduction

Python has established itself as one of the world’s most influential and widely-used programming languages. According to the 2025 Developer Ecosystem Survey by JetBrains, Python ranks as the second most used language, with 57% of developers reporting its use in the past 12 months. Even more impressive, over one-third (34%) of developers now consider Python their primary programming language, surpassing JavaScript, Java, and TypeScript in primary usage.

Why Python Stands Out

1. Simple and Readable Syntax

One of the biggest reasons behind Python’s popularity is its clean, readable syntax. Python uses English keywords and intuitive structures, making it exceptionally beginner-friendly. The 2025 State of Python report revealed that 50% of respondents have less than two years of coding experience, and 39% have been coding in Python for two years or less – proving Python is the ideal gateway for new programmers.
